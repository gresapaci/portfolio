About

I'm a Computer Science graduate who enjoys the point where clean interfaces meet clear code.

I graduated from Universum International College with a focus on frontend development, web technologies, UI/UX design and artificial intelligence tools.

My technical foundation covers HTML, CSS, JavaScript and Vue.js, supported by PHP, Laravel, Node.js, MySQL, Docker and API integration. For design and site building I work in Figma, WordPress, Elementor, Webflow and Framer.

For more than a year I have taught frontend development at a digital technology training center, which has sharpened how I explain ideas and structure code that beginners can follow. I also teach WordPress and Figma. I enjoy building responsive websites, designing intuitive interfaces and turning new tools into practical solutions.
